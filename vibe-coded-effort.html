General notes

  1. Feature percentages are of the vibe-coded build's own effort (the 100% baseline) and stack left to right, in the order checked.
  2. Each hardening bar is drawn to the same scale as the feature bar below it — its width (and so its area) shows that item's effort as a share of the checked feature total, not an extra stretch of the bar's length.
  3. Ranges shift with the app's shape: an internal single-user tool sits at the low end of every item; a customer-facing product with real money or regulated data sits at the high end of most.
  4. This drawing is the buildout only. Once launched, operating, administering, and supporting the app costs a similar order of effort on an ongoing basis — that's a separate estimate, not included here.
